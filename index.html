🐢 Toronto subway · Lines 1 & 2

How slow is your commute today?

Pick your trips once. An Apple Shortcut then checks the TTC's reduced speed zone list and tells you how many are on your route and roughly how much time they add.

🚇 Work: 2 slow zone(s), about +0.6 min
🐢 St Clair West → Dupont: 25 km/h instead of 65, +25 s
🐢 Union → King: 20 km/h instead of 36, +13 s

1Your trips

Add each trip you take, in the direction you ride it. Transfers between Line 1 and Line 2 are worked out for you.

2Get the shortcut

Tapping the button copies your trips, then downloads the shortcut. When Shortcuts asks for your trips, paste.

Copy trips & get the shortcut
  1. Open it on your iPhone. If Safari saves it to Downloads, tap the download to open it in Shortcuts.
  2. Tap Set Up Shortcut, paste your trips into the box, then Add Shortcut.
  3. On the first run, tap Allow when it asks to connect to ttc.ca.

3Check anytime

Runs the shortcut with the trips on this page, handy after you change them. Trips are saved in this browser only.

Check my trips now
  1. Every morning: Shortcuts → Automation → Time of Day → pick a time and weekdays → Run Immediately → choose TTC Slow Zones.
  2. With Siri: say “TTC Slow Zones”.
  3. One tap: in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen to keep this page as a launcher.
Trips as text (advanced)

This is exactly what the shortcut gets. To change trips later without this page, edit the first Text action in the shortcut.

The shortcut reads the TTC's public Reduced Speed Zones page directly on your phone each time it runs; this site doesn't collect or republish any data. Delay estimates only count time spent at reduced speed, so the real delay is usually a little longer. Always check the TTC for official service information.
